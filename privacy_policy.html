Privacy Policy for ZenWedge

Effective Date: August 1, 2026

At ZenWedge, accessible from ZenWedge application, one of our main priorities is the privacy of our visitors. This Privacy Policy document contains types of information that is collected and recorded by ZenWedge and how we use it.

1. Information We Collect

ZenWedge is designed with privacy at its core. We store your timer statistics (such as completed session counts and total focus time) locally on your device using encrypted preferences. We do not collect or transmit personally identifiable information to external servers.

2. Local Data Storage

All app preferences, session records, and sound settings are saved securely on your device storage. You retain full control over this data, which can be cleared at any time by clearing app data.

3. Third-Party Services

ZenWedge may use standard analytics or optional integration features (such as Gemini sound analysis or Firebase sync if explicitly enabled by user configuration). These services adhere to their respective privacy policies.

4. Children's Information

ZenWedge does not knowingly collect any Personal Identifiable Information from children under the age of 13. If you think that your child provided this kind of information on our app, we strongly encourage you to contact us immediately.

5. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Thus, you are advised to review this page periodically for any changes. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page.

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions or suggestions about our Privacy Policy, do not hesitate to contact us at support@zenwedge.app.
